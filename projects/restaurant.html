Marlow & Co. Café

A warm, inviting website concept for a neighbourhood café, built around the menu, the space and easy table reservations.

IndustryRestaurant / Café
ServicesWeb Design, Development, Menu Design
RoleDesign & Development
StatusConcept, 2026
Hero screenshot Replace with a full-width screenshot of the homepage hero

Description

Marlow & Co. is a neighbourhood café concept, built around the menu, the space and an easy way to reserve a table. The layout leans warm and inviting, a contrast to the darker, bolder style used across the other concepts on this site.

Challenge

A café website has to do two things at once, make the food look good and make it simple to find hours, location and a way to book a table.

Solution

A clean, image-led layout with the menu and reservation link placed high on the page, so a hungry visitor gets an answer fast.

Services Provided

Web design, frontend development, a designed digital menu layout and a basic SEO structure for local search.
