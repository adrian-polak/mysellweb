Forge Strength Studio

A high-energy website concept for a personal training studio, designed to turn visitors into booked trial sessions.

IndustryGym / Fitness
ServicesWeb Design, Development, SEO Structure
RoleDesign & Development
StatusConcept, 2026
Hero screenshot Replace with a full-width screenshot of the homepage hero

Description

Forge Strength Studio is a personal training concept, built around one goal: turning a first-time visitor into a booked trial session. Every section on the page works toward one action, booking a trial.

Challenge

Gyms and personal trainers often compete on price alone, since most sites look interchangeable. Forge needed a confident, premium-feeling site, matching the level of coaching on offer.

Solution

A high-contrast, energetic layout with clear pricing and a single, repeated call to action, book a free trial, placed at every natural pause in the page.

Services Provided

Web design, frontend development, a basic SEO structure and a landing page built specifically for the trial-booking flow.
