FAQ

Common questions

How long does a website take?
Most landing pages take one to two weeks from approved design to launch. Larger, multi-page sites usually take three to five weeks, depending on how much content you provide and how quickly you review drafts.
Do I need to provide the website text?
In most packages, yes. You know your business better than anyone. I'll guide you on what each page needs and help tighten up the wording, but the core content comes from you. Copywriting gets offered as an add-on if you'd rather I write it.
Will my website work on mobile?
Every site gets built and tested on desktop, tablet and mobile before launch. Responsive design isn't an add-on here, it's part of the process from the first sketch.
Can you redesign my existing website?
I do. Send a link to your current site and I'll give an honest take on what's working, what needs to change and what a redesign would involve.
Do you provide hosting?
Not yet. I'll help you choose and set up a hosting provider, such as Netlify, Vercel or a standard host, and hand over files ready to deploy. Ongoing hosting management gets discussed for larger business packages.
Can you create graphics as well as the website?
I do. It's part of what makes this studio different: website and graphic design come from one person, so your visuals stay consistent everywhere your business shows up.
How many revisions are included?
Each package lists its included revision rounds, usually one or two. Extra rounds get billed separately, with the cost agreed before any extra work starts.
